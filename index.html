Help with Ink Chaos

Two players, one iPhone Duo. Get your next ink duel going.

Start a match

  1. Open your iPhone Duo and choose Let’s play.
  2. Place four creatures on each side of the board and confirm.
  3. Choose a splat size and aim on your side. Fold the phone and reopen it to fire across the board.

Take turns until one team is painted. Your opponent can get a final shot to catch up. If both teams finish in the same number of turns, ink left and then aim break the tie.

Common questions

What do I need to play?

Two people sharing a compatible iPhone Duo running iOS 27.1 or later. Play uses the physical hinge. The game runs offline.

Why didn’t my splat fire?

Select a size you can afford, choose an aim point on your side, then fold and reopen the phone. Return to the game if you switched apps mid-turn.

How do I resume a game?

Pause and choose Save & home. Choose the resume option on the main menu to continue on the same device.

How do I change language, sound or vibrations?

Open Settings from the main menu. You can choose an app language or follow the language of your iPhone.
