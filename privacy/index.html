Privacy Policy

How Ink Chaos handles game data and support requests.

Effective 5 November 2026. Publisher and privacy contact: Robert Torbiczuk, robert.torbiczuk@gmail.com.

Data in the game

Ink Chaos runs offline and does not collect or send personal information, gameplay, or device information to the publisher. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Hinge input controls play on your device.

Local storage and deletion

The game stores match progress and settings on your device. A new match replaces the saved match. To remove the game’s local data, delete the app and its data. Apple may include app data in device backups according to your settings. You can manage those backups through Apple’s device and iCloud settings.

Help by email

When you contact support, the publisher processes your email address and whatever you choose to send to answer and resolve your request. Where the GDPR applies, this is based on the legitimate interest in providing support and handling disputes. The publisher keeps correspondence as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. Email providers handle delivery and storage on the publisher’s behalf.

Website and App Store

These help pages have no forms, advertising, or analytics scripts. The hosting service handles ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and requested page, to deliver and secure the site. Apple handles App Store purchases, downloads and optional system diagnostics according to Apple’s privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

Contact robert.torbiczuk@gmail.com to ask about or request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of support data, or to object to its processing where applicable. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
